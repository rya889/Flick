# Flick ads + audience pricing — 2026-10-01

**Audience:** Ryan Le / Forge  
**Persona re-validate:** Primary user = TikTok/Instagram-native who wants **more than brain rot** — anti-doomscroll **fiction finishers** (PD library, reels-style shorts, Listen/karaoke).  
**Locked:** Premium voices **inside** $4.99 Pro (simple ladder — no Voice add-on).  
**Prices (locked ladder):** Pro **$4.99/mo** · **$29.99/yr**. Lifetime later-only for non-metered UX.

Research compiled ~Oct 1, 2026 (ET). USD / US-centric unless noted.

## Launch default — v1 minimal ads (Ryan decision, 2026-10-01 ET)

Initial build should use the least frequent monetization possible so we do not scare users away. Keep the overall freemium model, but start sparse and add ads only after retention and momentum prove the habit.

- **Free at launch:** house Pro soft prompts only — at Finish end, in Settings, and as a voice-quality tease. **No third-party native shorts ads and no Finish interstitial at launch.**
- **Optional phase 1.5:** an opt-in rewarded premium-voice trial is acceptable only when the user taps for it. If that feels too early, defer it too.
- **Phase 2:** after retention/momentum, add native between-shorts ads and an after-Finish interstitial using the caps in §4.
- **Pro:** remains ad-free at **$4.99/mo or $29.99/yr**, with premium voices included.

This launch default supersedes the prior recommendation to turn on native and interstitial ads immediately; those placements remain Phase 2 planning guidance.

## 2. Persona re-validate (TikTok/IG anti-doomscroll finishers)

### Who they are
- Live in **TikTok / IG Reels / Shorts** muscle memory: vertical snack → swipe → dopamine.
- Want an **antidote**, not another endless void: finish a classic, feel progress, Listen on commute, karaoke-highlight moments to share.
- Overlap BookTok curiosity + Duolingo-style habit streaks + Wattpad “read in bites” — but Flick’s corpus is **free PD**, so willingness-to-pay is for **habit + immersion + voice + no friction**, not titles.

### Willingness to pay vs ad tolerance
| Signal | Implication for Flick |
| --- | --- |
| Gen Z: **~49%** would accept **2× ads** for cheaper streaming (Bango Subscription Signals; Millennials ~46%, all US ~36%) | Free-with-ads is culturally normal; don’t assume “young = paywall only.” |

| Wellness (Calm ~$14.99/mo / $69.99/yr; Headspace ~$12.99/mo / $69.99/yr) | Higher “painkiller” WTP. Flick is closer to **vitamin/habit** → freemium + lower price. |

| Wattpad Premium **from ~$4.99/mo** — #1 perk = **No ads** | Exact peer for fiction experience pricing. Validates $4.99. |

**Verdict:** Persona still maps to **$4.99 / $29.99**, not wellness $13–15/mo and not Serial’s $2.99 forever. Hybrid ads+sub matches how they already consume TikTok/IG and Wattpad.

| App | Ads | Sub / paid | Lesson for Flick |

## 4. Ad formats — what works in readers vs what destroys immersion

> **Phase 2 planning, not v1 launch:** the native and interstitial placements below are deferred until retention and momentum are established.

### Ranked for Flick

| Format | Use? | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Native card between shorts** | ✅ Primary | After every N shorts in vertical feed (start N=6–8) | Matches Reels/Threads sponsored rhythm; does not cut mid-clip. |
| **House ad → Pro** | ✅ Always-on soft | End of Finish, Settings, Listen quality tease | Zero eCPM but drives conversion; on-brand. |
| **Interstitial after Finish** | ✅ Sparse | Only after Finish celebration dismissed / “Continue” | Natural break (Play policy: end of content unit). Max **1 per Finish**. |
| **Rewarded video (opt-in)** | ✅ | “Try premium voice 15 min — watch a short ad” | Tapas/Duolingo-aligned; never forced. Caps trial COGS. |
| **Banner while reading** | ⚠️ Avoid at launch | — | Destroys immersion; CLS / thumb zone fights; Libby users will bounce. |
| **Mid-Listen / mid-karaoke audio ad** | ❌ Never | — | Breaks anti-doomscroll promise; trust killer. |
| **Mid-chapter / mid-paragraph interstitial** | ❌ Never | — | Wattpad’s hated pattern; don’t copy the pain. |
| **Pre-open book interstitial** | ❌ Never | — | Interrupts intent (Play “unexpected interstitial” risk). |
| **Rewarded to unlock PD chapters** | ❌ Never | — | PD must stay free; coins/WUF are for scarce Originals, not Gutenberg. |

### Frequency caps (Phase 2 defaults)
- **Native between shorts:** 1 ad per **6–8** shorts; no ad on first 2 shorts of a session.
- **Finish interstitial:** ≤ **1 per completed Finish**; ≥ **3 minutes** since any prior interstitial; skip if user finished &lt;2 min ago (avoid spam on short goals).
- **Rewarded:** user-initiated only; ≤ **3 rewards / day**; clear “Ad” label + reward copy before play.
- **Session soft cap:** ≤ **4** paid ad impressions / session (native + interstitial combined); house Pro cards don’t count.
- **Category filters:** block gambling, NSFW, scams, junk install ads — Duolingo lesson: filter even if eCPM drops.

### What NEVER to interrupt
1. Active **Listen** playback  
2. **Karaoke / highlight sync**  
3. Mid-**chapter scroll** / long-form reading  
4. First **open** of a book from library  
5. During the **Finish celebration** animation itself (ad only after user taps Continue)  
6. Onboarding / first-session aha (day-0 finishers)

## 5. Pricing: does $4.99 still fit? Tweaks?

### Keep
- **$4.99/mo** — Wattpad-tier experience price; under wellness & TTS giants; matches “cheap antidote to doomscroll.”
- **$29.99/yr** (~$2.50/mo, ~50% off) — **primary CTA** on paywall for this persona (budget-aware, annual-friendly when value is clear).
- **Premium voices inside Pro** (locked) — simple ladder; Speechify-level voice quality without Speechify price; TTS COGS managed with fair-use, not a second SKU.
- **Ad-free included in Pro** — same #1 Wattpad Premium perk.

### Tweaks (recommended, not ladder changes)
| Tweak | Launch? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Highlight annual** on paywall | ✅ Yes | Monthly still available; default toggle = annual. |
| **7-day free trial** of Pro | ✅ Yes (test) | Prove Finish + premium voice in one week; RC: many cancel day-0 — trigger trial after first Finish / meaningful Listen. |
| **Intro offer** ($0.99 first month → $4.99) | ⏸ Optional A/B | Use if trial underperforms; avoid training users to wait for sales. |
| **Student plan** (SheerID-style, e.g. ~$19.99/yr) | ⏸ Post-PMF | Headspace proves students convert with deep annual discount; adds support load — not launch. |
| **Raise to $5.99/$39.99** | ❌ Not now | No evidence this persona needs it; retest after retention proof. |
| **Drop to $2.99/mo or Serial-like lifetime** | ❌ No | Caps solo-founder income; voices-in-Pro needs recurring. |
| **Voice add-on** | ❌ Locked out | Voices stay inside $4.99 Pro. |

**Economics reminder:** Freemium Day-35 median paid conversion ~**2.1%** (RevenueCat 2026). Ads monetize the other ~98% without gutting the free moat. Annual emphasis improves cashflow; watch month-1 annual cancels (~35% of annual cancels in RC data).

## 6. Concrete launch default

### V1 free (initial build)
- Full PD library, basic read, basic Finish (1 goal), shorts consume (daily soft cap optional), on-device/system Listen, karaoke core.
- **House Pro soft prompts only:** at Finish end, in Settings, and as a voice-quality tease.
- **No third-party native shorts ads and no Finish interstitial at launch.** No mid-Listen / mid-read / pre-open ads.
- **Optional Phase 1.5:** user-initiated rewarded premium-voice trial only if the tap-to-opt-in experience feels ready; otherwise defer it.

### Pro — $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr (annual default on paywall)
- **Ad-free**
- **Premium voices** (fair-use / soft cap if cloud COGS)
- Unlimited Finish goals + richer stats
- Offline / sync / themes / shorts unlimited (per free/Pro matrix in pricing-research)
- Soft paywall after first Finish **or** N Listen minutes **or** day-3 streak — never block browse.

### Phase 2 (after retention/momentum)
- Add native between-shorts ads and an after-Finish interstitial, using the frequency caps in §4.
- Keep all interruption rules: never mid-Listen, mid-read, pre-open, or during the Finish celebration.

### Network / ops
- Keep third-party ad-network integration off the v1 critical path; use Remote Config when Phase 2 is enabled.
- Instrument house-prompt exposure/conversion and retention from day one; add ad_impression, ad_fill, rewarded_complete, finish_then_ad_shown, and free-user holdouts when Phase 2 starts.

### Copy anchors
- House prompt: “Finish deeper. Listen better. Go Pro — zero ads.”
- Pro: “Finish deeper. Listen better. Zero ads.”
- Optional rewarded: “15 min premium voice — watch one short ad.”

## 7. Ads + Pro interaction (locked rules)

1. **Remove ads = Pro perk** (alongside premium voices — not instead of).  
2. Pro users never see third-party or rewarded prompts (house tips OK, rare).  
3. Free users who complete rewarded voice trial get a **timed Pro-voice taste**, then soft paywall — not permanent unlock.  
4. Never gate PD chapters behind ads or coins.  
5. When Phase 2 begins, if ad retention tax &gt; ~3–5 pp on D7 vs holdout, **cut the interstitial first**, then native; keep only the least disruptive opt-in rewarded test.

---

## 8. Open / revisit triggers

- Revisit student SKU after &gt;1k Pro trials.  
- Revisit banner only if native eCPM collapses **and** retention holds in test.  
- Revisit price $5.99 only after annual attach &gt;40% of new Pro and D30 retention healthy.  
- Start Phase 2 only after retention/momentum is healthy; kill the Finish interstitial if Finish completion rate drops.
