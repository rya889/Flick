## 1. Bottom-line recommendation

**Model: Freemium + subscription (primary), with optional lifetime “Pro unlock” for non-metered features.**

| SKU | Price | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flick Pro — Monthly** | **$4.99/mo** | Default convert path; matches Wattpad-tier “experience” pricing, undercuts TTS giants |
| **Flick Pro — Annual** | **$29.99/yr** (~$2.50/mo) | Cashflow + retention; ~50% off monthly |
| **Flick Pro — Lifetime** (optional, later) | **$49.99** one-time | Unlock themes / Finish UX / offline / shorts polish; **exclude** cloud premium TTS (or soft-cap) so LTV isn’t destroyed by voice cost |
| **Pro Voice add-on** (if cloud TTS burns $) | **+$2.99/mo** or included in Pro with fair-use cap | Only if on-device TTS can’t carry quality wedge |

**Why this beats pure lifetime or hard paywall for Flick**

- Closest spiritual competitor (**Serial Reader**) sells a **$2.99 lifetime** IAP — great goodwill, weak solo-founder income. Don’t copy that ceiling.
- Content competitors charge **$5–12/mo** for *catalog access* (KU, Wattpad Premium+). Flick’s corpus is free → must charge **less than content subs** and **sell the finish/listen/shorts loop**.
- Pure TTS apps (Speechify **$29/mo / ~$139/yr**) prove people pay for *voice quality*, but that price is too high for “free classics” positioning and invites “why pay for Gutenberg?” churn.
- Hard paywalls convert ~5× freemium in RevenueCat 2026 data — but they fight Flick’s wedge (browse free books → habit → pay). Freemium fits the product story; use a **strong soft paywall** after first Finish or first Listen session.

**Expected economics (order-of-magnitude, not a forecast)**  
After Apple/Google cut (30% → 15% Small Business / year-2 subs): ~$4.25–$4.25 net on $4.99 monthly at 15%, ~$3.50 at 30%. At **2% freemium→paid** (RevenueCat freemium Day-35 median ~2.1%) and **$30–40 annual ARPU** blended, you need thousands of engaged installs for meaningful part-time income — so prioritize **retention of finishers** and **annual SKU**, not ad-driven MAU.

## 3. Industry patterns (reading + TTS)

1. **Subscription wins when value renews monthly** — new catalog, ongoing cloud TTS cost, sync, social feed, Finish streaks that need live server. Content and TTS leaders are almost all recurring.
2. **Lifetime works when cost-of-goods ≈ 0** — local reader chrome, themes, fonts, offline file openers (Moon+, Serial Premium). Risk: one payment, years of support; weak for ongoing AI voice bills.
3. **Freemium median conversion is low** — RevenueCat *State of Subscription Apps 2026*: freemium Day-35 download→paid median **~2.1%** vs hard paywall **~10.7%** (~5×). Retention after 1 year is similar (~27–28%). Freemium = slower cash, better fit for “free books” story.
4. **Annual is cashflow, not guaranteed LTV** — RC 2026: ~**35% of annual cancellations** happen in month 1 (users buy then kill renew). Still push annual for solo runway.
5. **“Why pay for free books?”** is beaten by paying for **habit + quality audio + offline + no friction**, same as Wattpad Premium (ads/offline) and Serial Premium (read-ahead/sync) — never by locking Gutenberg titles.
6. **Platform tax** — 15–30% App Store / Play. Model net revenue. Android involuntary churn from billing failures is high (RC: ~31% of Play cancellations) — enable grace periods / dunning early.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| **“Why pay for free books?”** | Never lock titles. Market Pro as Finish Coach + Listen Studio + Shorts. Copy: “The books are free. Finishing them isn’t.” |
| **App Store / Play fees (15–30%)** | Prefer annual; enroll Apple Small Business / Play equivalents; track net ARPU. |
| **TTS COGS** | Prefer on-device / OS voices on free; meter or fair-use premium cloud voices; lifetime must **not** include uncapped cloud TTS. |
| **Churn / Day-0 trial cancels** | Soft paywall after aha (Finish or Listen highlight sync); 7-day trial if testing; annual win-back in month 1. |
| **Libby / Gutenberg / Apple Books free path** | Compete on finish loop + shorts + karaoke sync, not catalog size. |
| **Serial Reader price anchoring at $2.99** | Differentiate with Listen + shorts + Finish; don’t race to $2.99 lifetime as primary SKU. |
| **Bandwidth (solo + job search)** | Ship freemium Pro with 1–2 paid pillars first (Listen quality + Finish goals); defer TLDR and heavy social. |
| **Radish-style coin fatigue** | Avoid micropay chapter unlocks on PD content — wrong fit and high support cost. |
